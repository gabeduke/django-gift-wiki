Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: March 2026

If you would like to delete your account and all associated data from Gift Wiki, follow these steps:

How to Request Deletion

  1. Contact the app administrator by emailing gabeduke@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include the email address associated with your account.
  3. Your account, wishlist items, and profile data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your user account and profile
  • All wishlists and gift items you created
  • Your profile picture (if uploaded)

What Is Not Affected

  • Gift items in other users' wishlists that you may have marked as purchased — those records will be anonymized rather than deleted, to preserve wishlist integrity for other family members.

For questions, contact the administrator at gabeduke@gmail.com.
