Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Gift Wiki is a private family gift exchange tracker. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: Your name and email address, provided via Google or Facebook login (through Firebase Authentication).
  • Wishlist data: Gift items, categories, and purchase status that you create within the app.
  • Profile picture: If you upload one (optional).

How We Use Your Information

  • To identify you within your family group and display your wishlist.
  • To allow family members to coordinate gift purchases.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Data Storage

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database (Neon) and optionally in S3-compatible storage for media files. The app runs on Google Cloud Run.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication — handles login via Google and Facebook.
  • Google / Facebook — used only for authentication; we receive your email and display name.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data. See our data deletion instructions.

Contact

This is a private family app. For questions or concerns, contact the app administrator.
